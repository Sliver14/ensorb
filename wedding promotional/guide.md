# VIDEO CREATION BRIEF: PREMIUM WEDDING WEBSITE SHOWCASE

Create a professional 55–60 second promotional video for InsightNovaTech, a software development and digital solutions brand.

## OBJECTIVE

Showcase a completed custom wedding website as a real portfolio project. Demonstrate how the website combines beautiful wedding storytelling with practical digital features. Attract couples, wedding planners, event professionals, and business owners who need custom websites or digital solutions.

This should look like a premium software agency's product showcase, not a generic wedding slideshow.

## VIDEO FORMAT

- Primary format: vertical 9:16
- Resolution: 1080 × 1920
- Duration: approximately 58 seconds
- Platform compatibility: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn
- Visual quality: clean, polished, professional, mobile-first

## CREATIVE DIRECTION

Use an elegant, romantic, modern and premium visual style.

Brand-inspired colors:
- Burgundy
- Blush pink
- Mint green
- Olive green

Use refined serif headings, clean sans-serif supporting text, subtle floral accents, tasteful color transitions, soft lighting, smooth camera movements, and understated motion graphics.

Avoid excessive gold, flashy effects, busy backgrounds, childish animations, and overly dramatic transitions.

## IMPORTANT FOOTAGE RULES

Use my uploaded wedding website screen recordings as the source of truth for the actual product.

Do not invent website features, change the website's interface, fabricate checkout screens, or generate fake versions of its pages.

Use AI-generated visual elements only as supporting transitions, backgrounds, or decorative elements. The actual website must remain clearly visible.

Do not invent customer testimonials, conversion statistics, or business results.

## SCENE-BY-SCENE STRUCTURE

### Scene 1: The hook | 0–6 seconds

Begin with an elegant, dark burgundy background with subtle blush particles or floral line-art motion.

Reveal the actual wedding website on a laptop and smartphone mockup.

On-screen headline:
"More Than a Wedding Website"

Supporting text:
"A beautiful digital experience for your special day."

Use a gentle camera push-in and a refined title reveal.

### Scene 2: The homepage and love story | 6–15 seconds

Transition into the actual homepage recording. Show the couple's names, visual design, and the Our Love Story section.

On-screen text:
"Your Story. Your Style. Your Day."

Use smooth scrolling and restrained highlights to draw attention to the interface.

### Scene 3: Aso Ebi color guide | 15–23 seconds

Show the actual Aso Ebi color guide.

On-screen text:
"Keep Everyone in Style"

Use subtle color-swatch motion or a clean animated frame around the real color guide. Do not replace the website's actual colors with invented ones.

### Scene 4: Gift registry | 23–32 seconds

Show the real gift registry interface and available gift options.

On-screen text:
"Make Giving More Meaningful"

Use a soft transition and restrained emphasis on the real registry features.

### Scene 5: Email invitations and RSVP | 32–42 seconds

Show the actual email invitation and RSVP flow.

On-screen text:
"Invitations and RSVPs, Simplified"

Use a subtle email-envelope animation followed by the real website footage. Do not imply that an invitation has been delivered unless the demonstration is genuine.

### Scene 6: Product summary | 42–49 seconds

Show a quick, well-paced montage of the homepage, love story, color guide, registry, and RSVP experience.

On-screen text:
"One Website. A Complete Wedding Experience."

### Scene 7: InsightNovaTech call to action | 49–60 seconds

Transition to a clean, premium branded closing screen.

Display:
"Need a Website Built Around Your Vision?"

"Custom Websites | Web Applications | Business Solutions"

"Let's Build Something Exceptional."

"InsightNovaTech"

Add my actual logo and contact information if provided. If no contact details are supplied, leave a clean placeholder for my website or social media handle.

End with a gentle logo reveal and enough time for viewers to read the call to action.

## VOICE-OVER

Use a warm, confident, natural English-speaking voice. The delivery should sound like a technology professional presenting a completed project, not a wedding invitation announcer.

Keep the narration conversational and synchronized with the scenes. Leave brief pauses between key phrases.

## MUSIC AND SOUND DESIGN

Use soft, elegant instrumental background music with a modern premium feel.

Keep music below the narration. Add only subtle transition sounds where appropriate. Avoid loud cinematic impacts or distracting sound effects.

Use properly licensed music suitable for commercial social-media use.

## SUBTITLES

Add accurate, synchronized captions. Use high-contrast text, readable sizing, short phrases, and safe margins so captions are not obscured by platform interface elements.

## FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Ensure the actual website remains the focus, every feature shown is real, the captions are spelled correctly, the voice-over is clear, and the closing brand and call to action are readable.

Export the finished video as a high-quality MP4. If possible, also provide a version without captions so platform-specific captioning can be added later.
